## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes turn-of-the-century print ephemera with early industrial telegraphy, cold-type letterpress, and mechanical dispatch hardware. It captures the tactile materiality of a late-19th-to-early-20th-century newsroom or telegraph exchange—dense ink on fibrous paper, heavy cast-iron machinery, and mechanical split-flap telemetry.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Material Authenticity:** Grounded entirely in aged newsprint, ragged deckle edges, carbon-dense ink, and telegraphist stamps. There are no glossy gradients or frictionless software tropes; surfaces hold grain, weight, and mechanical resistance.
- **Editorial Neo-Brutalism:** Combines dense Victorian broadsheet layout discipline with bold, unyielding neo-brutalist containment—3px to 4px structural black rules, hard ink and vermilion cast shadows, and tight grid lines.
- **Industrial Mechanical:** Accented by split-flap solenoids, knurled brass fittings, telegraphic monospaced readouts, and punchy linocut badges.

### Target Audience & Tone
Engineered for editorial publications, design archives, distinctive portfolio platforms, and specialized cultural products where personality, typographic craft, and historical gravity outweigh generic corporate neutrality. The emotional response is immediate: tactile nostalgia, journalistic authority, and mechanical delight.

## Colors

The palette operates on the physical chemistry of printmaking and cast metal. Light surfaces simulate unbleached rag paper and newsprint; dark tones represent lampblack carbon ink; accents evoke aniline stamping ink and patinated brass telegraph keys.

### Color Roles
- **Paper Canvas (`paper-base: #eadfc6`):** The primary tactile background. Never pure white; saturated with the warm yellow-brown undertone of aged broadsheet.
- **Secondary Sheet (`paper-muted: #d9ccad`):** Used for interior containers, tabular surfaces, and recessed panels.
- **Carbon Ink (`ink-deep: #16120f` / `neutral_color_hex`):** The structural line work, primary typographic text, heavy solid borders, and machine chassis framing.
- **Stamp Red (`stamp-red: #b8281c` / `primary_color_hex`):** Aniline postal red used for commanding woodblock headings, critical CTA triggers, active notifications, and high-impact drop shadows.
- **Tarnished Brass (`brass-gold: #c8963e` / `secondary_color_hex`):** Warm metallic accent representing telegraph dials, rating ornaments, linocut rules, and gilded typographic flourishes.
- **Machined Umber (`tertiary_color_hex: #6a5c48`):** Mid-tone mechanic brown for secondary rules, metallic chassis insets, and technical metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs two distinct historical technologies: high-contrast editorial serif broadsheets and ribbon typewriter/telegraph mechanical feeds.

### Pairing Strategy
- **Display & Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Treated with the dramatic vertical presence of hand-carved woodblocks and hot-metal editorial types. Titles run tight, authoritative, and unapologetic. Large headlines alternate between Carbon Ink and Stamp Red to punctuate sections like morning extra editions.
- **Body & Technical Readouts (`Courier Prime`):** Serves as typewriter monospaced prose. It mimics dispatch wires, mechanical manifests, and editorial leads.
- **Eyebrows & Kicker Badges:** Monospaced, capitalized, and tracked out with wide letter-spacing (`0.18em` to `0.24em`) to simulate linotype operator header tape.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to the structure of a multi-column broadsheet gazette set against an underprinted 30px × 30px drafting grid pattern (`rgba(22, 18, 15, 0.08)`).

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (12 Columns, Max 1200px):** Content organizes into disciplined newspaper columns (1-col dispatch metadata, 2-col sidebar, 4-col feature card, 6-col editorial lead, or full 12-col masthead). Gutter is strictly `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (6 Columns):** Sections reflow into symmetrical double-column blocks with a `1.5rem` gutter.
- **Mobile (2–4 Columns):** Columns compress into a single-column stacked telegram wire format with a `1rem` gutter and `1.25rem` screen margins.

### Rhythmic Discipline
Vertical pacing follows strict multiples of `0.5rem` (8px). Editorial sections are separated by expansive breathing room (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`) punctuated by physical filigree rules, linocut divider ribbons, or deckle torn-paper boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects soft, blurred, ambient shadows. Depth is communicated strictly via **hard-offset flat neo-brutalist cast shadows** and **mechanical bevel insets**, evoking physical woodblocks and punched brass plates.

### Shadow Architecture
- **Layer 0 (Flat Paper Sheet):** No shadow. Sits flush on the grid canvas.
- **Layer 1 (Stamps, Tags, Buttons):** Hard cast shadow: `3px 3px 0 var(--ink-deep)`. Hover states extend to `6px 6px 0 var(--ink-deep)` with an upward-left translation of `-3px, -3px`. Active pressed states snap back to `1px 1px 0 var(--ink-deep)`.
- **Layer 2 (Broadsheet Cards & Modules):** Hard vermilion shadow: `8px 8px 0 var(--stamp-red)`. Hover lift transitions to `12px 12px 0 var(--ink-deep)`.
- **Layer 3 (Featured Posters & Master Folios):** Hard stamp shadow: `12px 12px 0 var(--stamp-red)`.
- **Mechanical Insets (Split-Flaps & Dial Housings):** Crisp inner shadows simulating cut-out plate apertures: `box-shadow: inset 2px 2px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4), inset -2px -2px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
- **CRT / Phosphor Emissives:** For telegraph monitors, text uses concentrated warm amber glow: `text-shadow: 0 0 10px rgba(238, 210, 150, 0.6)`.

## Shapes

The primary shape language is **sharp (0px roundedness)**, reflecting the geometry of guillotine-trimmed newsprint, letterpress lead furniture, and machined metal dies.

### Shape Rules
- **Panels, Cards, and Containers:** Strict 90-degree squared corners (`0px`). Structural continuity is reinforced by solid 3px or 4px carbon ink perimeter frames.
- **Exceptions (Functional Mechanical Details):**
  - **Telegraph Keys & Circular Badges:** 50% circular pills reserved solely for numbered indices, sequence steps, and brass rotary dials.
  - **Punched Stamp Perforations:** Radial scallop cutouts along card edges (`mask-image` repeating circles).
  - **CRT Viewing Portals:** Elliptical curvature (`border-radius: 12px / 8px`) specifically bound to analog screen previews.

## Components

### Buttons & Dispatch Triggers
- **Primary Stamp Button:** Sits on `--stamp-red` with bold monospaced uppercase typography in `--paper-base`. Outlined with a 3px solid `--ink-deep` border and a hard `4px 4px 0 var(--ink-deep)` drop shadow. On hover, translates `-2px, -2px` with shadow expanding to `6px 6px 0`.
- **Secondary Dispatch Button:** Parchment background (`--paper-base`), `--ink-deep` text, 3px solid `--ink-deep` border, backed by a `4px 4px 0 var(--stamp-red)` shadow.
- **Telegraph Link:** Inline typewriter text with a thick dotted carbon underline (`border-bottom: 2px dashed var(--ink-deep)`), shifting to solid stamp red on hover.

### Postal Badges & Metadata Chips
- Encased in a 2px solid `--ink-deep` rule with `--paper-muted` background fill. Micro-tracked monospaced typography (`label-sm`).
- Key tags can feature decorative notched corners or simulated ink bleed outlines using procedural turbulence filters.

### Form Inputs & Telegraph Fields
- **Input & Textarea:** Inset newsprint panels with background `--paper-muted`. Bordered with a 3px solid `--ink-deep`. Text entered displays in `Courier Prime` at `body-md`.
- **Focus State:** 2px dashed `--stamp-red` outline with an `outline-offset: 3px`, reinforcing the look of a scored perforation line. No soft focus rings.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-pressed box forms (18px × 18px), 3px solid `--ink-deep`. Selected checkboxes feature an authoritative "X" mark inked in `--stamp-red`. Radios are filled with a solid circular dot.

### Cards & Broadsheet Folios
- Constructed with a 4px solid `--ink-deep` border and an 8px hard drop shadow in `--stamp-red`.
- Interior header separated by a 2px horizontal carbon ink rule or a 3px double rule (`border-bottom: 3px double var(--ink-deep)`).
- Card footers feature category stamps and issue numbers set in tracked monospace caps.

### Lists & Dispatch Timeline Steps
- Ordered lists use circular badge counters (50% border radius, 28px diameter), filled with `--stamp-red` and outlined with 3px solid `--ink-deep`, casting a `2px 2px 0 var(--ink-deep)` shadow.
- Step lines are connected vertically with a centered 2px solid `--ink-deep` telegraph rule.

### Mechanical Split-Flap Readouts
- Horizontal flip tiles divided by a 1px deep center hinge score. 
- Top half tinted slightly lighter parchment, bottom half shadowed by 10% black overlay.
- Character transitions trigger a crisp 3D perspective fold (`rotateX`) with an overshoot spring curve (`cubic-bezier(0.3, 1.4, 0.5, 1)`).
